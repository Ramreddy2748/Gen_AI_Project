## Comparison to baseline (GPT-4o-mini, same prompt and data)

| Metric | GPT-4o-mini | DeepSeek-chat | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Window accuracy | 60.53% | **62.50%** | +1.97 |
| Window fall recall | **39.13%** | 15.25% | −23.88 |
| Window fall F1 | **46.15%** | 26.09% | −20.06 |
| Video accuracy | **66.67%** | 63.16% | −3.51 |
| Video fall recall | **29.17%** | 12.50% | −16.67 |
| Video fall precision | 77.78% | **100.00%** | +22.22 |
| Video fall F1 | **42.42%** | 22.22% | −20.20 |

At the video level, GPT-4o-mini caught 7 of 24 falls; DeepSeek caught only 3 of 24. DeepSeek made zero false positives — but at the cost of 21 missed falls. For an elderly-care safety system this is the worst possible failure mode: the model has effectively learned to refuse to predict "fall" out of caution.

## Why CoT collapsed even further on DeepSeek-chat

The window-level fall predictions dropped from 117 (GPT-4o-mini) to 20 (DeepSeek), an 83% reduction. Both models exhibit the same pattern — when forced through five explicit reasoning steps before answering, a non-reasoning LLM tends to talk itself into the safer "NO_FALL" answer. DeepSeek-chat is a strong general model but, like GPT-4o-mini, is not reasoning-tuned, so the multi-step prompt amplifies rather than mitigates this conservatism. The result is high precision (90% / 100%) and very low recall, the inverse of what's wanted for a safety-critical detector.

## Takeaway

CoT with `deepseek-chat` is unsuitable for this task. The recommended next experiment is the same prompt against `deepseek-reasoner` (DeepSeek R1), which is reasoning-tuned and is far less likely to collapse into conservative answers when prompted with explicit step-by-step reasoning. The cross-model pattern observed here — CoT recall drop on both OpenAI and DeepSeek non-reasoning models — is itself a finding worth reporting: prompting strategy interacts with model class (reasoning vs non-reasoning) more than with model brand.
